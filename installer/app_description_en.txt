GearWin - Complete PC Care

Complete tools for maintaining, cleaning and improving your PC.

A free, open-source maintenance and optimization tool for Windows 11, built to bring everything a
user needs to keep their PC fast, clean, secure, and under their own control into one place -
no more hunting through dozens of separate tools or manually editing the Windows registry.

The app is organized into 9 tabs:

* Home - Your PC's overview at a glance: system health score (with a trend sparkline and a S.M.A.R.T.
  disk health badge), quick maintenance actions, disk space analysis, and network connection status
  with a live throughput graph.

* Updates & Apps - Scan once for updates to apps, Windows and drivers from several trusted sources,
  install them in bulk, remove built-in Windows components you don't need, or install recommended
  apps with one click.

* Cleanup & Performance - Switch Office, Gaming or Ultimate Performance modes, free RAM instantly
  (with a real before/after number), clean temporary files and caches, and tame what starts with
  Windows.

* Health & Repair - Check and repair system files (SFC/DISM), the disk (CHKDSK) and WinRE, clean the
  registry safely (with an automatic backup before every change), and follow your disk health over
  time.

* Security & Privacy - Run a Quick Scan with Windows Defender, review firewall rules and open ports,
  block Microsoft telemetry, manage browser passwords locally, and reset or tune the network (DNS,
  hosts) in one click.

* Games & Multimedia - Your Steam/Epic/GOG/Ubisoft/Xbox library with launch, per-game GPU/gaming profiles and launcher-cache cleanup; display, audio and camera/microphone checks, codec detection, and a video/audio converter with batch conversion.

* Windows Tweaks - Dozens of Windows interface/performance/privacy tweaks, toggled with a single click, with
  safe restore to the original value, custom right-click menu entries, and named or file-based
  settings profiles you can save and switch between instantly.

* Backups & Reports - Export reports (PDF/TXT, diagnostics ZIP, battery report, problem bundle) and
  manage restore points, registry backups and driver backups in one place.

* System & Tools - See a full analysis of your hardware under "My Device", then use the tools: full
  maintenance in one click, the Windows toolbox, WSL, USB history and more.

Extras: a system tray icon with quick actions (Quick Clean, Health Check, live CPU/RAM/network mini
popup) and an optional always-visible desktop widget, 21 visual themes with Light/Dark modes and
animated backgrounds, a "Windows Classic" and "Microsoft PC Manager" skin, and automatic detection of
your Windows display language on first launch.

Fully supports 14 languages (Greek, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Russian, Chinese,
Japanese, Portuguese, Korean, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi), and is distributed for free under the MIT License. A few
individual features rely on separate open-source libraries under their own licenses (see the License
page of this setup) - full details in the app's own Help > License tab.
